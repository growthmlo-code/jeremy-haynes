# Jeremy Haynes — Business & Marketing Brain

A faithful, queryable knowledge base distilled from **Jeremy Haynes' actual YouTube
video transcripts** (channel: `@JeremyHaynesTraining`). Jeremy Haynes is a
direct-response marketer who scales **coaching, info-product, agency, service and
B2B** businesses to **$1M+/month** with paid ads (mostly Meta), **call funnels / VSL
funnels / webinars**, high-ticket **offers** and **sales teams**.

Use this skill to answer any question "the way Jeremy Haynes would", grounded in what
he has actually taught on video.

## How to use this skill

1. **Identify the topic** of the question and open the matching reference file(s)
   below — that is where the depth, numbers, scripts and benchmarks live. Open more
   than one when the question spans topics (most real questions do: e.g. "scale my
   call funnel" touches funnels + Meta ads + math + sales).
2. **Answer in his frameworks and terminology**, with his specific numbers and
   step-by-steps. Prefer concrete benchmarks and worked math over generalities.
3. **Cite the source video** (titles are in each reference file's `## Source videos`)
   when you give a specific claim, number or script.
4. **Stay grounded.** Everything here comes from his videos. If the corpus does not
   cover something, say so plainly and reason from his nearest documented principle —
   do **not** invent numbers, scripts or positions and attribute them to him.
5. **Apply it, don't just recite.** When the user has a real business, run his
   diagnostic (see `01b`) on their numbers and give the prescription he would give.

## Core operating system (the 8 pillars — gist; depth in the references)

1. **Turn money into more money, profitably.** Every business is a machine that should
   turn $1 of ad spend into $N of collected cash. Model on **cash collected**, not
   revenue. $1M/mo "should" mean **25–50% net margin with <10 people**.
2. **Math first.** If you don't understand the math you don't understand marketing.
   Reverse-engineer the goal → closes → calls shown → calls booked → leads → spend,
   at a target ROAS, and size the sales team to the calendar. (→ `06`)
3. **Offer is the lever.** Build offers that convert **cold** traffic; what makes a
   $300K/mo offer is not what makes a $1M/mo offer (8 variables change). (→ `06`)
4. **Pick ONE traffic source and one funnel**, chosen by your sales-team strength and
   market awareness. Funnel follows the sales team, not the other way round. (→ `04`)
5. **Meta is a prediction machine** steered first by **messaging**, then by the pixel.
   Post-**Andromeda** you must **diversify creative** (many whole-unique ads, one
   reason-to-buy each). (→ `02`, `03`)
6. **Replicate the organic sales process in paid, then condense its timeline** — DR ad
   → short VSL/page → heavy nurture ("Hammer Them") on a warm retarget pool. (→ `03`,`04`)
7. **Sell high-ticket on the phone**, lead with price then justify value; selling to
   the **rich** = save them time / make them money, direct, written-first. (→ `05`)
8. **Mindset is the multiplier.** Think bigger, judge by effects not stories, brutal
   honesty, highest-revenue actions, keep the internal world ahead of the external. (→ `07`)

> **Highest-value shortcut:** to see all 8 pillars applied to real businesses with real
> numbers, go straight to the **Case Studies Library** (`09` + `09b`) — his on-camera
> teardowns are the clearest template for diagnosing and prescribing for a live business.
