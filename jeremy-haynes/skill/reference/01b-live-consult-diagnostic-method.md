# How Jeremy Haynes Diagnoses a Business (Live Consult Playbook)

A reusable diagnostic method extracted from Jeremy Haynes' live business breakdowns,
where he sits across from an owner (agency, coach, lead-gen, info) doing $115K–$1.5M/mo
and finds what is actually stopping them from the next level. This is the **repeatable
pattern** — the questions, the sequence, the levers — not a per-video recap.

Everything below is grounded only in the six transcripts listed under "Source videos."
Short verbatim quotes are cited to the video they came from.

---

## 0. The core lens he views every business through

Before any tactic, he collapses the business down to one function: **turning money into
more money.** He forces the owner to see themselves as a money manager, not a
marketer/coach/operator, because it reframes pricing, risk, and what counts as a real
problem.

- "You're modern-day money managers... flipping money into more money at a more aggressive rate than almost every single traditional way." [Watch Me LIVE Scale This $246K/Month Advertising Agency In 64 Minutes]
- "All you're actually doing is just turn money into more money. That's what people are giving you money to do." [Watch Me LIVE Scale This $246K/Month Advertising Agency In 64 Minutes]
- "Paid advertising is the ability to just condense everything that happens organically into a much shorter period of time." [This $400K/Mo Business Asked Me How To Hit $1M/Mo (Live Consult)]

Two premises follow that recur in every consult:
1. **Build for the destination, not for now.** "You always want to build from the intention of where you actually want to get to." A $1M/mo business is a different build than a $500K/mo one. [Here's How I'd Take This $300K/Mo Sales Coach To $1M/Mo (Live)]
2. **Behavior is downstream of reasons.** Weak reasons → stall. This is why he opens with the goal and interrogates it.

## 1. The intake sequence (the questions he asks first, in order)

He runs the same interrogation nearly verbatim across all four business types. The order
matters: revenue → goal (and commitment test) → margin → offer/price → **cash collected**
→ traffic → funnel → capacity.

1. **"How much you grossing each month right now?"** Then he pins it down: is that USD? Is that the peak or the average? ("Is 400 the peak?" / "convert that to USD for us.") [This $400K/Mo…] [What This $1M/Mo Business Should Do Next To Scale (Live Breakdown)]
2. **"What's your goal / revenue goal?"** — and he immediately stress-tests commitment. Hesitation is itself a finding: "You see how unsure you are and how undecided you are." [Here's How I'd Take This $300K/Mo Sales Coach To $1M/Mo (Live)]
3. **"What are you netting out of that?"** (margin %, and whether COGS/fulfillment is counted).
4. **"What's the actual offer? What's it cost?"** — then tiers and the % revenue split across tiers ("What's the percentage makeup of this 330K…").
5. **"What's your AOV? Do you actually know what you cash collect up front?"** He separates contract value from **cash collected now**, and treats a vague answer as a red flag. [This $400K/Mo…]
6. **"How you getting your deals right now?"** (traffic channel — organic vs paid, which channel dominates, and whether one is a *discovery* channel feeding another's *nurture*). [Here's How I'd Take This $300K/Mo Sales Coach…]
7. **"What funnel are you driving traffic to?"** (DMs, VSL call funnel, webinar, quiz/application, direct scheduler).
8. **Capacity questions:** "How many salespeople? Are they maxed out? How fast can you fill a new rep's calendar? How long to hire+train one?" — used to expose whether the constraint is the *channel* or the *team*.

Throughout, when an owner says "we're *trying* to…" he kills the word: "There's no such
thing as trying. You do or you don't… If I said to pick that trophy up, you just pick it
up." [Helping This $1M/Month Lead Gen Business Scale In 78 Minutes]

## 2. How he finds the bottleneck

He uses three overlapping diagnostics. He isolates **the single most contracted thing**
and attacks only that — never everything at once.

### a) Funnel bottleneck analysis (the stat-by-stat walk)
He recites the whole funnel as a chain of statistics and asks which one is broken:

> "Doing a simple bottleneck analysis like are my CPMs good, is my link click-through rate good, is my cost per call good… what's my show rate, close rate, AOV." [This $400K/Mo…]

Reference benchmarks he gives on a cold webinar/call funnel (from the $300K sales coach consult):
- **Cost per lead** = CPM (≈$30–80) × link CTR (target ~2%) × opt-in rate (20–50%); good CPL single digits, realistic ~$15–25, above $30 usually doesn't work.
- **Show rate** (cold): 30% great, 20% average, 10–15% "something's fucked up."
- **Webinar retention to pitch:** 80%+.
- **Booking rate at pitch:** ~30%.
- **Show rate to those calls:** ≥70%. **Close rate:** ≥35%.
- "You just look at which specific statistic is contracted… isolate your attention exclusively to that one… then attack it." [Here's How I'd Take This $300K/Mo Sales Coach…]

### b) Capacity / constraint analysis (is it the channel or the team?)
When an owner wants to *add a channel*, he checks whether the existing channel is actually
maxed or whether the **sales/setter team** is the real ceiling:

- "You've maxed out your reps. That's what you've actually maxed out." [What This $1M/Mo…]
- He reverse-engineers hidden constraints: a 9x webinar that decayed to 2x correlated to *closer availability*, not the webinar — "What was the sales capacity at that given time?" [What This $1M/Mo…]
- He warns 100% capacity makes reps *worse* (no follow-up, no CRM, "buy or die culture"); target ~80–90%. [What This $1M/Mo…]

### c) Cash-timing / tracking analysis (how long are you fronting cash?)
He probes whether they know their payback — how long from ad spend to profit in pocket,
renewal rate, and time-to-deliver:

- "You have no idea how long it takes for you to pocket your profit." [Helping This $1M/Month Lead Gen Business…]
- Missing tracking/finance is treated as a core failure, not a side issue — hence his CFO recommendation to the lead-gen team.

## 3. The prescription sequence (the order he fixes things)

1. **Find the domino** — the one change that makes everything else fall into place. "What's the one thing specifically that we can do that makes everything else just fall into place right after." [Watch Me LIVE Scale This $246K/Month…]
2. **Pick the highest-probability / highest-confidence bet first**, and delineate every option by confidence before betting money. "Exclusively action the things that you are the most confident in first." [Here's How I'd Take This $300K/Mo Sales Coach…]
3. **One thing at a time, with a 30-day commitment.** New things feel exhausting because no neural pathways exist yet; doing many at once compounds the exhaustion. Commit a full month of honest effort before judging. [Here's How I'd Take This $300K/Mo Sales Coach…]
4. **Improve → change funnel → change offer** (escalating order). First augment/improve what exists via bottleneck analysis; only after rigorous trial-and-error change the funnel *type*; the biggest change (and last resort) is the offer itself. [Watch Me Give A Room Of 50+ Entrepreneurs My Top $1M/Month Lessons]
5. **Don't let the client add complexity.** He ends by making them repeat the plan and warns: "Don't take what I'm saying and add more complexity to it. Literally do what I said." [Watch Me LIVE Scale This $246K/Month…]

## 4. The levers he pulls most often (cross-business patterns)

These show up again and again regardless of business type:

- **Raise price / cash collected up front.** His single most common move. More cash up front → profitable immediately, client takes it seriously, and you get breathing room to fix stats. "Charge 12K up front… you're immediately break even." He ties low AOV to the owner being scared to ask for the full amount. [Watch Me LIVE Scale This $246K/Month…] [Here's How I'd Take This $300K/Mo Sales Coach…]
- **Dynamic, leverage-based pricing.** Price per deal based on who has leverage, not one static rate. Charge *more* on small/risky deals (same effort, higher risk): "Takes the same amount of time… to do a tiny deal as it does to do a big deal." [Watch Me LIVE Scale This $246K/Month…]
- **Expand the ICP / broaden the market.** His most repeated strategic lever. Stop talking only to the narrowest buyer:
  - agency owners → all high-ticket business owners (his own story) [Here's How I'd Take This $300K/Mo Sales Coach…]
  - info-only → any high-ticket product/service [Here's How I'd Take This $300K/Mo Sales Coach…]
  - selling to salespeople → selling to the *businesses* that employ them (more money, easier to close) [Here's How I'd Take This $300K/Mo Sales Coach…]
  - fitness trainers → personal brands in general (the Brian M. example) [What This $1M/Mo…]
  - Rule: "Don't talk to the floor… talk to the top of the umbrella." You still catch everyone below. [This $400K/Mo…]
- **Dedicate / expand sales capacity to the real opportunity.** Put your *best* closers on the highest-value offer; build a separate team for the new offer; work the decaying wait-list ("$3 million of decaying revenue potential"). [What This $1M/Mo…]
- **Open a second acquisition channel — but only the right one.** Webinar vs VSL call funnel is chosen by *sales-team strength* and *market awareness*, not preference (see §6).
- **Backend selling systems + upsells + continuity.** Confirmation pages with breakout videos, value-dense email sequences carrying the "epiphany moments," an AI clone, continuity after the term ends ("does sales training really just stop after six months?"), and real upsells. [This $400K/Mo…] [Here's How I'd Take This $300K/Mo Sales Coach…]
- **Efficiency before hiring.** Maximize productivity per role, hire A-players who do many jobs, be result-oriented not time/effort-oriented, and test AI (AI SDR, CRM follow-up agent) before adding headcount. "I never hire people unless there's a 100% trackable, this person's paying for themselves." [Watch Me LIVE Scale This $246K/Month…]
- **Install financial/tracking rigor.** Know payback period, renewal rate, time-to-deliver; hire a CFO/finance analyst when cash is being held and fronted at scale. [Helping This $1M/Month Lead Gen Business…]

## 5. The mistakes he repeatedly calls out

- **Reasons gassed out.** Stalling = reasons too weak to justify the sacrifice/risk. "Your reasons gassed out." Strong reasons must weigh on you *now*, not in the future. [This $400K/Mo…] [Watch Me Give A Room Of 50+ Entrepreneurs…]
- **Either/or thinking (the classic 100K–300K mistake).** Choosing sales *or* fulfillment. "Sales to a highly successful business is breathing… as soon as you stop breathing you've got about 2 minutes." [This $400K/Mo…]
- **Fear left unactioned.** Fear is a signal of where to strengthen, not a reason to stop. "Fear is essentially an indication of what we need to strengthen" — like a check-engine light. [Here's How I'd Take This $300K/Mo Sales Coach…]
- **Loss aversion / sunk-cost clutching.** Refusing to drop a working-but-capped offer or funnel. Cam took 3 months to add a webinar because he clutched the old call funnel. [Watch Me Give A Room Of 50+ Entrepreneurs…]
- **Running a lifestyle business while claiming to want scale.** "This isn't going to generate serious wealth for you the way you're running it right now." [This $400K/Mo…]
- **Low-impact vs high-impact work.** Mistaking activity for progress. "There's a difference between low impact work and high impact work… doing the right work that's high impact is exclusively what matters." 90% actions vs 10% "dinker" actions. [This $400K/Mo…] [What This $1M/Mo…]
- **Mis-framing paid ads as high-risk.** Applying organic's long cycle to paid. Paid = gambling you control and get data from; find the "Goldilocks zone" of spend — enough to take it seriously, not enough to ruin you. [This $400K/Mo…]
- **Under-betting / halfassing the bet.** Spreading a monthly budget thin across many small tests divides seriousness and upside. "What you spend the most on is what you take the most seriously." [This $400K/Mo…] [Watch Me LIVE Scale This $246K/Month…]
- **Over-complicating everything.** "You guys over overcomplicate the fuck out of everything." Especially at 100–300K/mo. [Watch Me LIVE Scale This $246K/Month…]
- **Self-imposed ceilings from key beliefs.** Narrow ICP, refusing a channel (IG, shorts), fearing fulfillment/reputation — each is a belief blocking the obvious action. [What This $1M/Mo…] [Here's How I'd Take This $300K/Mo Sales Coach…]

## 6. Decision frameworks he reuses

**Webinar vs VSL call funnel** — decided by two things:
- *Sales-team strength:* layup-trained "cashier" reps who can't run education calls → webinar; if the offer is stronger than the reps, a VSL call funnel works. "If your offer is stronger than your salespeople, you can run with a VSSL call funnel." [This $400K/Mo…]
- *Market awareness/education + sophistication:* educated/in-market buyers → call funnel; buyers needing education or an unrecognized mechanism → webinar; saturated markets need a **unique mechanism**. [This $400K/Mo…] [Here's How I'd Take This $300K/Mo Sales Coach…]

**Improve vs change-funnel vs change-offer** — escalate in that order; only abandon after rigorous trial-and-error (see §3.4).

**Testing bandwidth vs financial bandwidth** — two separate yes/no questions before launching: do you have the cash to endure testing, and the *operational attention* to fix it? If attention is thin, run only the single highest-probability funnel. [This $400K/Mo…]

**Trigger-based scaling** — test budget per event (e.g., $7,500/webinar); two positive-ROI runs in a row = a trigger to scale the next one; aim ≥3:1. [Here's How I'd Take This $300K/Mo Sales Coach…]

**Ad-account testing structure** — Thunderdome (one ad per ad set, 3-sec exclusion) to rapid-test and expose losers and scale winners *in place*, vs moving winners into a scaling CBO where they must re-compete; always run redundant accounts/pixels/pages (risk mitigation at scale). [Helping This $1M/Month Lead Gen Business…]

**Offer-narrative/timing fit** — an offer rips when it matches the story the market is already telling itself (the Cole Gordon closer-placement timing; placement offers are "hot"). Ask: have they tried and been burned by the alternative, making now the moment? [This $400K/Mo…]

## 7. Patterns by business type

- **Agency ($246K/mo):** reframed as money managers; raise cash collected up front (12K setup fee) to be profitable immediately and escape ad "learning mode"; charge dynamically by leverage; efficiency/A-players/AI over headcount; flip referral-dependence to paid so they can say no to wrong deals; high-leverage AI upsells (CRM follow-up agent). [Watch Me LIVE Scale This $246K/Month…]
- **Sales coach ($300K/mo):** expand from salespeople to the businesses that employ them and from info-only to all high-ticket; fear of fulfillment/reputation is the lynchpin block; add continuity + AI clone + real upsells; start paid with a weekly cold webinar (his strength), bottleneck-analyze it. [Here's How I'd Take This $300K/Mo Sales Coach…]
- **Lead-gen ($1M/mo):** product/ops/finance business, not a marketing one — fix tracking and hire a CFO (know payback, renewal, time-to-deliver); tiered priority/dedicated systems for top spenders at higher price; Thunderdome + Hydra lead-form tests + redundant ad accounts; the moat is speed-to-qualified-close. [Helping This $1M/Month Lead Gen Business…]
- **Info / coaching ($400K & $1M/mo):** behavior/reasons work dominates; stop either/or (never stop selling); lean fully into the richer demographic the data already favors (Brian's new offer/wait-list); break troughs with *multiple* high-impact actions at once (new product + new channel + double output); just place the bet (launch the webinar/VSL now, not in two weeks). [This $400K/Mo…] [What This $1M/Mo…]
