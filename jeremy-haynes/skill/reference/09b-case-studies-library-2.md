## [Bryson — sales-recruiting / recruiter-branding agency (DFY content + backend system), started at $114K/mo] — "He Went From $114K/Mo To $278K/Mo In 3 Months (Business Update)"

- **Starting situation & numbers:** Last appearance (≈3–4 months prior, ~June/early Q3) was $114K/mo after recovering from a contraction that had dropped him "below a 100K a month." Now last month "came right short of 300… like 278." Gross = ~$278K. Net: won't disclose, says "very very healthy margins." Core offer: **$25K down + $7K/mo, 6-month minimum** (25K + five payments of 7K); just raised to **$30K down + $8K/mo**, 6-mo min. Second offer (content only, no DFY backend): **$18K + $6.5K/mo** — "two people to do it ever." Of the $278K, essentially all was the 25K/7K offer ("literally one" deal on the lower offer). Niche: sales recruiting for direct-to-home (door-to-door pest control, solar) and especially insurance; TAM "tens of thousands." Organic alone got him to ~$170–180K/mo, then he turned ads back on (had to relearn ads after losing his ads-running business partner). Ad metrics: **cost per call ~$1,000** (cold); **RoRoAS 7.38x on cash collected** in August; **spent $24,000** that period (not blended with organic). Warm retargeting reached only **19,000 of ~1,000,000** possible people and drove the majority of revenue; cold "got me like two closes right at the beginning and then… never converted another person." Same funnel to cold + warm (not split), so cold-vs-warm ROAS unknown. Creative director paid **$15,000/mo**; everyone else "less than 15." Recently hired a setter at **$8K/mo base + 5%** of closed deals.
- **The bottleneck he identifies:** He had **turned his ads off ~4–5 days earlier** — cutting his own acquisition/revenue source — because he hit fulfillment capacity while training new content staff. "Every time you cut off the ads, you reduce your leverage."
- **His diagnosis (why):** An "all-in or all-out" personality that can only focus on one thing at a time; a need for control that makes him shut off revenue instead of using leverage (waitlists, ascension offers). Jeremy exposes the circular logic: Bryson said he shut ads off because he "couldn't take on more deals," then admits he "went super hard on organic" to sell *the same offer* — "how the [__] that circle jerk logic make any sense?" "What actually kept your revenue low last month? Turning off the ads."
- **The prescription (in order):** 1) **Turn ads back on and scale the spend** — a 7:1 RoRoAS is a "you problem, not a strategy problem." 2) **Never reduce leverage** — keep ads on at capacity by waitlisting ("put the bat signal up") and selling a lower "ladies watch" offer (half-circle analogy) so he can keep acquiring. 3) **Duplicate the funnel to split cold vs warm** — unique URL, unique application, unique video (for play-rate/engagement stats), plus UTM tracking, to learn true per-audience ROAS; "easy to set up." 4) **Build the full ascension stack** before assigning who sells it: an up-offer (DFY ads for clients — he's done it free for a few and must quantify net dollars made before pricing) and a down-offer (DIY/done-with-you group coaching ~$6,800 built on his content software + a closer in training). 5) **Hire A-player talent and pay for it** — "you can't… tell me you're hiring the greatest people… at 278K a month"; big tech pays engineers ~$300K/yr + stock; pay immediate incentives like his setter deal.
- **The math / projected outcome:** Worked cost-per-call example: $1,000/call × 5 calls = $5,000 for 5 calls; 3 of 5 show; close 1 → $5,000 CAC vs $25,000 cash collected = 5:1 — and his actual is 7.38x, so better than that. Therefore he could tolerate a **$2,000 cost per call at 3–4x** and still "scale the hell out of" it. Leverage demonstration: he hit **$1M/mo recurring** in the inner circle, capped new full-circle entry, and when a member asked him to move mastermind dates he dropped/replaced that member "within 19 minutes." No projected revenue figure stated for Bryson's own next step beyond "scale the spend."
- **Transferable lesson:** Entrepreneurship's purpose is to make your dreams materialize *faster* than a job would; willfully slowing down (shutting off acquisition) lengthens the painful part of the cycle. Find and deploy the leverage in every situation; "expand as a man" so you can run everything at once instead of all-in/all-out. "The amount of money we make dictates the speed that life materializes."

## [Cameron England — AI-agent automation consulting (+ a Medspa marketing agency he dislikes), ~$500–700K/mo] — "Watch Me LIVE Fix This $500K/Mo AI Automation Business In 69 Mins"

- **Starting situation & numbers:** 20 years old. Last month ~$700K, this month pacing ~$500K ("slower month"). Goal: **$1M/mo** (modeled to hit it this month, ads didn't cooperate). Margins: last month **58%** (~$260–270K net on $700K); this month ~**30%** (ad unprofitability). Mix of the ~$500K main offer last month: **30% paid ads, 25% referrals, 20% YouTube, ~20% other organic** (friends/family blended into referrals → ~30%). Overall business ~**60–70% backend** (renewals/upsells/payment plans) vs 30–40% front end. A separate **Medspa marketing agency does ~$200K/mo** (co-founder has 30% equity; Cameron "hates" it — churn-prone liability). **AI offer:** $1K paid audit → custom build **$15–50K** (by revenue/complexity; partner has 30% equity, does all fulfillment; 90-day build window + account manager; post-90-day upsell = fractional AI dept **$2–4K/mo** OR placement: charge client **$6K/mo**, pay specialist $3–4K). Funnel: VSSL → application → separate scheduler page. Ads this month: **spent ~$71K**, **front-end RoRoAS 1.3** ($93K new cash on $70K); finishing ~$90K spend; net RoRoAS ~breakeven. **Cost per call $330.** Application→schedule ~**80%** (only 20% drop — strong; separate app+scheduler usually ~50% drop). **First-call show rate 40%** (constraint). **First-call close rate 17%** (closing the $1K audit). Second call (onboarding/sales hybrid): booking ~90%, show ~81% (likely ~100%). Second-call close on full build ~**65–70%** (7 of ~11–13 ad-sourced deposits). **15-day avg sales cycle.** Audit AOV $1K; build upfront cash ~$8K avg (this month higher ~$11K: 5 paid in full, 2 paid $5K); **cost per full build $10K** this month. **Opt-in rate 1.5%.** CPMs / link CTR unknown.
- **The bottleneck he identifies (corrected live):** He *thought* it was CRM/sales-team + show rate. The real earliest-in-funnel bottleneck is the **1.5% opt-in rate**, plus a **willful blindfold** on core analytics (doesn't know CPM, CTR, or opt-in rate on demand).
- **His diagnosis (why):** Cameron is "**Chad ramming**" his scale — spending more blindly on confidence — which puts him in "a hyper state of reactivity" with almost no proactive thinking time (**"5 to 10 minutes… and then on a Sunday 30 minutes"**). Moving too fast means his attention is reactively controlled; nobody in the org proactively owns the bottleneck stats.
- **The prescription (in order):** 1) **Fix the opt-in rate first** (earliest metric, "the closest metric to the top of the funnel that will unlock the rest"). 2) **Likely delete the opt-in page** — it's "artificial inflation" of cost per call; "9.9 out of 10" it's not justified when selling to rich buyers; verify by pulling the CRM report of opted-in-but-didn't-apply who still bought. 3) **Get CPMs + link CTR** (could have a 0.2% CTR / $400 CPMs and not know it). 4) **Split aware vs unaware audiences** — Google Trends shows almost nobody knows "AI agent"; run separate campaigns/funnels/VSSLs/backends; "the ad concept… determines what audience it reaches" (true since Andromeda). Match front-end messaging to the pattern of actual closers (**~90% already used Claude/open-source; just lacked time**). 5) **Hire an all-star marketing helper (~$15–20K/mo)** to own bottleneck analytics proactively. 6) **Build a scaling financial model** (spend→calls→closers-needed) and hire closers *ahead* of the spend. 7) **Question the 90-day fulfillment window** — could it be 30? Reclaim 1–2 hrs/day of proactive time.
- **The math / projected outcome:** Opt-in 1.5%→3%→6%, with every downstream stat held constant, **doubles returns twice** (4x total efficiency). If the 1.3 RoRoAS became **3x on $90K spend → ~$270K upfront cash**. At 30–58% margins even a $500K down month nets "somewhere around 175 to 200 at least," making a $15–20K/mo analytics hire a "100% worth it" no-brainer.
- **Transferable lesson:** Speed buys momentum but manufactures reactivity; buy back proactive time by hiring A-players to own the stats you're scaling into. Fix the earliest broken funnel metric before optimizing anything downstream (domino). Don't chase a step (show rate) when the top of the funnel (opt-in) is the real leak.

## [Brandon — Meta + Google Ads done-with-you workshops for service businesses (Australia + US), ~$1.4M USD / $2M AUD per month] — "What This $1.4M/Mo Business Should Do Next To Scale (Live Breakdown)"

- **Starting situation & numbers:** First-ever $2M AUD month (~$1.4M USD). Net ~**20%** average; last month **12.5%** ("gassing ad spend to try and hit that 2 mil") — 12.5% of $2M "I was pretty happy with." Offer: **2-day workshop + 6 months support = 7.2K (AUD)**; drop-cell online-only version **5.4K** (~2/3 take the 7.2K). After the 6 months it rolls into **$450/mo** ongoing support; **churn ~6%** (wants ~4%). Also **~$30K/mo recurring** as a top High Level affiliate. Customers: entry-level service/trades business owners (~$30K/mo avg; contractors ≈1/3; rest coaches/consultants); "usually they're tripling at least." Closes **~220–250 new clients/month** (AU + US). Acquisition: FB/IG **call funnel**. Spend **~$18K AUD/day Australia + ~$10K AUD/day US** (~$16–17K USD/day total). **RoRoAS: AU 2.5, US 1.5** (past peak 3.5x). AU cost-per-call ~**750 AUD** (~$400 USD); show rate **60–65% AU / 45–50% US**; close rate **45% AU / 30% US**. CPM ~**100 AUD AU** (~$60 USD, fine) vs **~211 AUD US** (~$120–130, high). Link CTR **1–1.2%** (want 2%). **Opt-in rate 4%** on first page; calendar page **55–60%** (≈50% drop app→scheduler). Day-one cash collected ~**4K AUD** (~$2.5K USD; 1/3 upfront, 2/3 payment plan). **Recurring = only ~100K AUD/mo (~$60K USD) of the $2M.** Spent **$3M+ on AU ads over ~2 years** against a TAM of ~750K business owners (reaching maybe ~400K); only **4 webinars** in that span.
- **The bottleneck he identifies:** Jeremy names several, led by this being a **purely transactional business that neglects recurring/backend revenue** — recurring is "100%" under-prioritized ("you don't prioritize it at all"). He also calls his sole revenue funnel a **"spare time activity"** with no dedicated owner, and page conversion is **~half** the benchmark.
- **His diagnosis (why):** Brandon treats his lead funnel ("$1.4 million a month in revenue") as a side task done "in our spare time" by just him + Aiden, while over-focusing on fulfillment. He built a **6-month cash-velocity cycle** on himself before the $450/mo second money even starts — "you created this entire game… you are the game maker," and he gave himself 180 days to produce results with no reward/milestone for faster growth. Poor people think "either/or"; rich people do both.
- **The prescription (in order):** 1) **Funnel split test** — add webinar and low-ticket/book funnels vs the lone call funnel; run AI analysis on 2 years of ad data to find best hooks/messaging that *also absorbed meaningful spend* (not just lowest CPM / highest CTR). 2) **Hire funnel owners / channel owners** (~$10–20K/mo each + ~2.5% of that funnel's revenue) so each funnel gets full-time attention. 3) **Collapse the cash-velocity cycle** — replace the fixed 6-month unlock with **milestone-based** unlocks so fast achievers reach (and pay for) advanced content sooner; charge **more than $450/mo** for the genuinely-more-advanced second-6-months content. 4) **Add real upsells** — a **triple-tier workshop model** (à la the "Mosi"/Hormozi model): an intimate higher-priced second workshop (e.g., **20 people × $10K**) and a **1-on-1 coaching** option (~$50K). 5) **Get-richer training** to cut churn (clients leave because "it worked" or they plateau — "tall poppy syndrome"). 6) **Fix page conversion** (double it to hit benchmark) and lift ad CTR toward 2%.
- **The math / projected outcome:** ~**3,000 new customers/year**. If **10% (300 people) paid $20K extra** → major upside; if **5% (150) bought** a higher-level program → more. Recurring "could be the entire business" and should be ~half of revenue if clients were retained/ascended rather than left to leave. One extra workshop per quarter (already runs 7/quarter) transacts them again before the 6-month mark.
- **Transferable lesson:** You are the game-maker — don't give yourself 6 months to deliver when 30 days is possible; "second money is the easiest money." Build milestones (celebrated, rewarded) rather than static timelines. Do BOTH marketing and fulfillment ("rich people… do both"). Teach clients to get richer so they don't plateau and hand their next dollar to a competitor.

## [Kurosh & Michael (Tooth Traffic) — lead-gen + call-center appointment setting for dental-implant practices, $246K/mo] — "Watch Me LIVE Scale This $246K/Month Advertising Agency In 64 Minutes"

- **The math / projected outcome:** They turn **$1 → $6–8 every 30 days**. A **$12K upfront + $5–10K ad spend = $17–22K bet**; 6x on $5K ad spend = **$30K** back in 30 days, so the bet is rational. Supporting AI examples: member "K" made **+$802,000 in June** from an open-claw CRM agent matching testimonials to objections across 400,000 DM conversations; Jeremy's own agent sends **~8,000 emails/day** and generated "**a little over 200[K]** trackable" from ~30,000 contacts. Analogy: "what you spend the most on is what you take the most seriously" (his $20K pinball machine; $552K Cullinan) — a $3,750 vendor isn't taken seriously.
- **Transferable lesson:** An ads agency is a **modern-day money manager** — price by leverage and by the client's upside, not by habit or your first deal. The **domino effect**: one change (collect enough cash upfront) makes profitability, scalability, and breathing room to optimize all fall into place. Efficiency and A-players beat headcount; "the risk is low, the upside is monumental… that's a no-brainer bet."

## [Tom — hyperbaric-chamber sales, AI-first business with zero full-time employees, $0 → $1M+/mo, >$10M/year, AOV $50K+] — "How He Built A $10M/Year Business With Zero Employees Using AI"

- **Starting situation & numbers:** Launched from **$0**, scaled to **$1M+/mo** and "a little over **10 million dollars**" over the year (~12M+/yr run-rate). **AOV $50K+** per chamber. **No full-time employees** — only a few one-and-done contractors. (This is a narrated case-study breakdown, not a live consult, so most funnel conversion metrics below are "not stated.") Margins / net / cost-per-lead / close rate / CPM / show rate / opt-in rate / ad spend / cash collected / NRR: **not stated.** Product line: single-seat → lay-down → quad-person chambers (chairs, TVs, red-light panels), varying sizes/colors; both physical on-hand inventory and made-to-order (months of lead time).
- **The bottleneck he identifies:** No live diagnosis — the video showcases how Tom removed the usual constraint of **scaling sales without a sales team / headcount** by designing an AI-first system.
- **His diagnosis (why):** A human-setter operation is limited: young setters are often non-compliant, can't train on *everything*, and don't follow up reliably. An AI setter trained on comprehensive data is fully compliant, "can actually train on literally every single thing you upload," and follows up contextually at scale.
- **The prescription (the system, in order):** 1) **Facebook instant form** with conditional-logic qualifiers — "$50K chamber, can you afford? Y/N"; No → drop-sell "$10–20K chambers, in range? Y/N"; No → booted (keeps the results column clean so the algorithm learns the right people); Yes → free-form open questions (AI-analyzed; **longer character count = higher intent, show rate, close rate, AOV**). 2) **AI setter** (Claude Opus 4.6 — "Claude is everything"; voice via Eleven Labs, originally Bland AI which had less latency; texting via Send Blue, tested Blue Bubbles) qualifies financially *and* raises interest, answering all product/pricing/delivery questions. 3) **"War room" training data in Notion** — categorized good/bad/average sales calls, deep product training, SOPs, real-time inventory + delivery ETAs. 4) **Tool access for the agent** — Salesforce (moves leads through pipeline, dynamically writes personalized email sequences), QuickBooks (quotes, invoices, cash-collection follow-up, customer service), Asana (agent task management). 5) When intent is high (criteria defined in the war room), the agent makes a **three-way text intro to Tom, who closes** ("generating himself layups all day"). 6) Infra: consolidated **6 Mac-Mini agents into one Mac Studio.**
- **The math / projected outcome:** No worked math; the stated result is **$0 → $10M+/year, $1M+/mo, AOV $50K+, zero full-time employees.** Jeremy notes only ~0.1% of people ever reach $10M/yr, and doing it AI-first with no employees is rarer still.
- **Transferable lesson:** An **AI-first business** can replace an entire setter team with one agent — if you feed it a comprehensive "war room" knowledge base (calls, product docs, SOPs, live inventory) and give it real tool access (CRM, invoicing, task mgmt). The AI qualifies, raises intent, and follows up contextually at scale; the human does only the final close. Free-form application answers (character count) are a usable intent signal the AI can act on.
