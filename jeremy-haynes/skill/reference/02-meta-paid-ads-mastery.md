Haynes' foundation is that you are not "targeting" — you are feeding a super-intelligent prediction engine and letting it decide.

- Meta is "a gigantic prediction machine of super intelligence" that predicts "who's probable to take this action the most." [The Most Valuable Meta Ads Training You'll Ever Watch]
- Meta claims ~**52,000 revolving data points per user** that update in real time. [The Most Valuable Meta Ads Training You'll Ever Watch] / [The New Era of Meta Ads Has Officially Begun]
- He cites a free Meta model, **Tribe V2**: they MRI-scanned tens of thousands of people scrolling, built an AI that "90 plus% of the time predicts exactly what part of the human brain is probable to fire off." [The Most Valuable Meta Ads Training You'll Ever Watch]
- Most advertisers fail because they "lack trust" in this machine and try to force control. Trust = let it put many ads in an ad set and spend on what it deems a winner.

**Why broad is now the default:** Meta ran a study — if they ignored bad advertisers' targeting and just reached the people their "trillion dollar prediction machine" knew would convert, everyone did better. "That's why advantage audiences exists. That's why broad targeting is the default." [The Most Valuable Meta Ads Training You'll Ever Watch]

## 2. What Actually Controls Targeting (the order matters)

Targeting is controlled by **two things, in strict order**: [The Most Valuable Meta Ads Training You'll Ever Watch] / [The Top Ad Strategies Working Right Now For Hitting $1M/Mo]

1. **Messaging** (+ the standard event / custom conversion you select) — controls *who you first reach* and *how long you stay in front of them*, before any pixel data exists.
2. **Pixel conditioning** — a *secondary* bias that rolls in over time based on who actually hits your results column.

### Messaging controls targeting first
- "Before there's any pixel conditioning data, messaging is what controls who you are probable to go after." [The Most Valuable Meta Ads Training]
- What controls targeting = **everything on the ad level + everything on the funnel/VSL itself**: video content, body copy, headlines, landing page copy, VSL script. "Yeah, they track that too." [The Most Valuable Meta Ads Training]
- The selected standard event/custom conversion adds additional bias to who it hunts.

### Messaging is "sensitive" (the JK Rowling lesson)
Remember one word: **sensitive**. A single word or handful of words wildly shifts who you reach. [The Most Valuable Meta Ads Training] / [The NEW Way To Scale Ads with Meta's Andromeda Update]
- A member teaching a copywriting course said in an ad you could "write copy just as good as famous authors like JK Rowling." The words *author* and *JK Rowling* flooded his funnel with wannabe authors — the wrong pocket. [The Most Valuable Meta Ads Training]

### Messaging pockets, oil fields & aquifers
- Each messaging angle has a **finite pocket** of convertible people plus a **replenishing rate** (new people entering/exiting the market daily). [The Most Valuable Meta Ads Training]
- **Scale ceiling** = the point where "every dollar that is spent above this amount is inefficiently spent." Stay at the ceiling; risk extra dollars on new tests. [The Most Valuable Meta Ads Training]
- **Oil field** = non-renewable pocket. Strike it, scale it, eventually run dry → cost per result flies. People then blame Meta; really "you just ran out of people that were probable to convert with that specific messaging." [The Most Valuable Meta Ads Training]
- **Aquifer** = a true scaled winner, replenished by a spring. If you extract faster than it refills, it also goes inefficient. True scaled winners "barely budge on lowering the profitability when you go to scale."

## 3. Pixel Conditioning — Myths vs. Reality

- **Pixels hold only 180 days of data** (confirmed by Meta), and operate on **recency bias** — the most recent ~2 weeks are weighted most in who it goes after next. [The Most Valuable Meta Ads Training] / [10 Years of Brutally Honest Facebook Ads Advice]
- "Stop operating under the illusion that you have a decade worth of pixel conditioning. You don't." [The Most Valuable Meta Ads Training]
- **Over-reliance on the pixel means your messaging sucks.** Classic tell: someone loses an ad account/pixel, relaunches identical ads in a fresh account, and suddenly attracts "donkeys" → proves the pixel was carrying weak messaging. [10 Years of Brutally Honest Facebook Ads Advice] / [The Most Valuable Meta Ads Training]
- "It's very dangerous to exclusively operate on the pixel by itself." Great messaging is your fallback when the pixel has issues. [The Most Valuable Meta Ads Training]

### Fixing a bad pocket (when wrong people start converting)
When donkeys start hitting your results column, they condition the pixel to find more donkeys (recency bias). Steps Haynes gives: [The Most Valuable Meta Ads Training]
1. **Withhold data from the pixel** — intentionally fall below the 50-events/week threshold so the algo hunts new pockets.
2. Switch firing from the auto web standard event to the **Conversion API**, firing manually only when the right person is confirmed (e.g., salesperson marks a qualified call).
3. Consider a **brand-new pixel**, or **duplicate the campaign/ad sets** and don't let the old data source report back.
4. Once the right people reappear, **open the floodgates** — shove as much data into the results column as possible to lock into the right pocket.

> Rule: "When I am in the wrong pocket, I'm withholding data. When I'm in the right pocket, I'm giving as much data to that results column as I possibly can." [The Most Valuable Meta Ads Training]

## 4. Standard Events, Custom Conversions & Cyclic Campaigns

- **Prefer standard events over custom conversions.** Standard events are used by almost all advertisers, so Meta "has more data per user on who's taken those specific actions in the past" → higher conversion probability, lower cost. Custom conversions are "isolated to your business" only. [The Top Ad Strategies Working Right Now For Hitting $1M/Mo] / [82 Minutes Of The Top Paid Advertising Strategies]
- **Optimize deeper down the funnel as volume allows.** Beginners optimize for the booked call (web pixel + conditional logic on the application). At scale (usually $2–5M+/mo) you optimize for a *qualified* call via Conversion API, firing only when the sales team confirms quality. [The Top Ad Strategies]
- Real case: an advertiser was optimizing for qualified + unqualified applicants (a custom conversion) at a **$400 cost per qualified call**; switching to a qualified **standard event** pushed it to **~$40** while spending ~$150K/mo. [82 Minutes Of The Top Paid Advertising Strategies]

### Cyclic campaigns (a scaling tactic)
- Your ads only reach the slice of your TAM likely to do *the specific event you optimized for*. To expand reach, duplicate a campaign and optimize one step up/down the funnel (e.g., one for `Schedule`, one for `Submit Application`). [The Top Ad Strategies]
- Think Venn diagram: pick two standard events that **don't overlap much** so each campaign reaches a mostly-unique audience, expanding total TAM.
- Don't launch cyclic at test budgets — it introduces ROAS/intent inconsistency between campaigns. It's a *scaling* tactic.
- Diagnostic: if a webinar campaign optimized for `Lead`/`Complete Registration` brings the wrong people, that's a **messaging** problem. If it's only profitable when optimized for `Purchase`/`Schedule` (delayed-firing, blank results column), that's **over-reliance on the pixel**. [The Top Ad Strategies]

> Learning phase requirement throughout: **50 reported events per week, per ad set** — "not just on the campaign level, each one of these needs 50." [The Most Valuable Meta Ads Training]

## 5. Testing: The Thunderdome (high-volume creative testing)

Use this **only if you don't trust Meta's distribution** and believe specific creatives deserve spend they aren't getting. [The Most Valuable Meta Ads Training]

**Setup:**
1. **One unique ad per ad set** (ad-set budget optimization / ABO). 30 ads → 30 ad sets.
2. Add a **3-second video-view exclusion** on the ad in each ad set → once someone sees that creative, they never see it again; they rotate to your other creatives. (Turn images into videos so the 3-sec exclusion applies.)
3. Targeting usually **broad** (interest stacks / lookalikes / warm audiences optional).
4. **Budget = total test spend ÷ number of ad sets** (e.g., $3,000/day ÷ 30 = $100/ad set/day).

**Operating it:**
- Early, the combined/summary cost per result looks inflated because you're forcing spend onto losers. Click into individual ad sets to see the true <10% that win.
- **Cut losers fast** (~3 days at a solid budget; otherwise give roughly one cost-per-result of spend before cutting). Funnel that spend into winners to **reveal each winner's scale ceiling rapidly**.
- Leave winners exactly where they're winning. Delete losers over time to open room for new test ad sets. Repeat.

**Win-rate reality (critical numbers):** [The Most Valuable Meta Ads Training]
- Win rate on the ad level is usually a **single-digit %** (≤9 of 100 ads win).
- **~10% or less** win — e.g., 3 of 30.
- **Only ~1–3% of 100 creatives are true *scaled* winners** (can absorb serious spend).
- Most winners have small scale ceilings ($100–$1,000/day); a tiny minority are your biggest winners ever.
- A "winner" is defined by **Meta**, not you — it's the ad that can absorb the most spend/scale, not necessarily the lowest cost per result.

**Scaling truth — don't split test & scale campaigns:**
> "Where a winner is winning is where it's probable to continue winning." Don't pull a winner out of a test campaign into a "scaling campaign" — it dramatically lowers the odds it keeps winning. [The Most Valuable Meta Ads Training] / [10 Years of Brutally Honest Facebook Ads Advice]

- **True ROAS doesn't exist at test budgets.** CPMs, CTR, page conversion all shift at scale → expect a slight cost inflation and a "**trough of scaling**." [The Most Valuable Meta Ads Training]
- Keep winners at their ceiling as **foundational campaigns** that keep chugging; accumulate winners over time.

## 6. THE ANDROMEDA EVOLUTION (pre- vs post-Andromeda)

This is the single biggest "what changed" in Haynes' recent material. Announced **April 22 (2025)**, fully rolled out to all accounts by **July** — "everybody got [hit] by it in July." [Meta's NEW Andromeda Update Is Skyrocketing Ad Costs]

### What Andromeda actually is
- Generative AI created ~**10,000× more ad creatives** than ever existed. Meta had to rework its creative recommendation process. [Meta's NEW Andromeda Update]
- Meta's current pipeline = **retrieval + ranking**: [The Top Ad Strategies] / [The NEW Way To Scale Ads with Meta's Andromeda Update]
  - **Andromeda = the RETRIEVAL step** (the new first step). It sifts "tens of millions, hundreds of millions… billions" of possible creatives down to a few thousand with the best context for the user.
  - **Lattice (2023) = the RANKING step.** It takes those few thousand and picks the exact ads for each placement (feed, reels, stories, Messenger), factoring advertiser budgets + conversion probability.

### Before vs. After Andromeda

| Dimension | PRE-Andromeda | POST-Andromeda |
|---|---|---|
| Creative volume | A handful of creatives; relaunch when fatigued | Must load **many diverse** creatives (see paths below) |
| The lazy shortcut | Film new hooks, keep same body + CTA, stitch together | Still "technically works" but **much lower probability**; Meta wants whole-ad diversity |
| What mattered most | The **majority hook** (one dominant hook, then second-order, then minor hooks) | **Tiny/niche hooks** now get distributed to micro-pockets |
| Distribution | Dominant hook showed again and again | A dominant hook still gets most reach, but when it fatigues it rotates to the **smallest hooks** that historically meant nothing |
| What Meta requires | — | **Ad diversification** — diverse *messaging* (and optionally themes + visuals) |
| Longevity | — | Diverse-messaging path lasts **longer**; cheaper CPMs |

> "Niche messaging is being distributed at a larger rate than majority hooks… in this post-Andromeda world." [The NEW Way To Scale Ads with Meta's Andromeda Update]

### What to do NOW — the two paths
Meta's own newsroom testimonial: a head of e-commerce went from "three to four creatives a week" to "**50 a week**" to maintain scale after Andromeda. [Meta's NEW Andromeda Update]

- **Path 1 — Volume: ~50 unique creatives/week (~200/month).** You *can* still use the lazy hook-swap method here. Downside: "ad fatigue on steroids… like antibiotics. When a disease becomes immune to it, grows stronger." More effort, burns through creative fast. [Meta's NEW Andromeda Update]
- **Path 2 — Diversity (preferred): ~15 unique creatives/month, each unique through and through.** Each entire ad is built around **one distinct reason to buy** (not just a new hook on the same body). Result: better CPMs, longer campaign longevity, cheaper cost per result, less account churn. [Meta's NEW Andromeda Update]

**How to generate diverse messaging (the dog-food exercise):** brainstorm every reason someone buys (healthier, dog prefers it, smoother coat, lives longer, fewer vet visits, happier…) — they built a graph of **30+ reasons**. Each reason = one whole, theme-consistent ad. [Meta's NEW Andromeda Update]

### Ad-set loading in the Andromeda era
- Load **25–30 unique creatives per ad set** (no shared bodies/CTAs). [The NEW Way To Scale Ads with Meta's Andromeda Update]
- Expect only **1–3 of them to get all the reach/spend**; the other ~22–27 get "barely any money… sometimes pennies." You still must launch all 25–30 to get Andromeda's benefit.
- **Refresh method:** when the winning 1–3 fatigue (pocket runs dry), *duplicate the ad set, turn off the 1–3 that already won*, and let the remaining 22–25 (that only got pennies) test against each other → reaches new pockets. Gets efficient after the first launch.

### Talking to a small pocket repeatedly
Articulate the same person from three angles — and keep "sensitivity" rule #1 in mind (don't say anything that attracts the wrong people): [The NEW Way To Scale Ads with Meta's Andromeda Update]
1. **Problems** the individual has
2. **Circumstances** they're in
3. **Outcomes** they want

For a large/multi-audience business, run the same problems/circumstances/outcomes exercise for *each* audience type.

**Tooling:** Haynes recommends **credit-based LLMs with an agent mode** (not flat-rate ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini) because they "read all the transcripts word for word" without efficiency guardrails, and agent mode can research real-world context for believable angles. [The NEW Way To Scale Ads with Meta's Andromeda Update]

**Watch for:** a Meta creative-solutions specialist tweeted about an alpha-test column flagging creatives "too similar to one another either visually or in messaging." Andromeda technically wants diversity in messaging themes **and** visuals; Haynes' testing found messaging is the main lever, but adding theme/visual variation helps if results lag. [The NEW Way To Scale Ads with Meta's Andromeda Update]

## 7. The New "Hyper-Aware, Hyper-Sophisticated" Algorithm (competitive density)

A separate algorithm shift (distinct from Andromeda's creative pipeline) made buyers swarm with competitors. [The New Era of Meta Ads Has Officially Begun] / [New Facebook Ads Algorithm Update Is Killing Sales]

### What changed
- As soon as a user shows interest (clicks / watches too long), Meta **swarms** them with 5–12 near-identical competitor ads until that interest is exhausted or the app is killed. [New Facebook Ads Algorithm Update Is Killing Sales]
- The mechanism = a **dart board**: Meta first throws varied concepts; once the user clicks one (the bullseye), it rotates through everything categorically similar, following them across feed/reels/stories/Messenger. [The New Era of Meta Ads Has Officially Begun]
- **Before:** clicking something surfaced "a handful" of loosely similar businesses (e.g., restaurants in general). **Now:** every one of the next 5–12 ads is the *same category* (e.g., all steakhouses) with no deviation. [New Facebook Ads Algorithm Update Is Killing Sales]
- Result in the real world: sales teams hear every qualified lead naming **2–5 competitors** they're considering — objections that never used to exist. Grounded via Breakthrough Advertising (Eugene Schwarz): markets are now forced to **high sophistication + high awareness** almost overnight. [New Facebook Ads Algorithm Update Is Killing Sales] / [The New Era of Meta Ads Has Officially Begun]
- **Cold targeting is now effectively broad** ("your targeting options are just suggestions"). **Warm audiences are still respected** — social interaction, video viewers, website visitors, email/customer lists are NOT treated as broad. [New Facebook Ads Algorithm Update Is Killing Sales]

### What to do about the swarm
- **Creative range is now survival.** A single ad = "you're [screwed]." Run a wide range of direct-response hooks/creatives **plus** content so retargeting is handled and you're present in the swarm. [New Facebook Ads Algorithm Update Is Killing Sales]
- Add **unique-mechanism content** and **process content** to your remarketing so closers can lean on something competitors lack. (Unique mechanism = *how* you get the result; process = all the steps leading up to it — the "steakhouse broiler + salt only" vs. "farm → dry-age → cut thickness → broiler.") [New Facebook Ads Algorithm Update Is Killing Sales]
- **Shorten the funnel cycle** (e.g., advertise a webinar no more than ~72 hours out; book calls closer in; start the education/sales cycle immediately on the confirmation page). [The New Era of Meta Ads Has Officially Begun]
- **Sell against alternatives** — treat every lead as already aware of all competitors; make your mechanism/process/pricing stance explicit; consider charging *more* to be the premium.
- **Five perspectives to audit how you stack up:** your own, your customer's (talk to good AND bad customers), AI's view of you vs. competitors, the algorithm's view (use burner accounts to see who you're *actually* served against), and funnel-hacking / secret shopping. [The New Era of Meta Ads Has Officially Begun]
- **Be everywhere** (multi-touch attribution): YouTube, IG, X, blog, your own AI, podcasts — present where competitors aren't. "It's always a multi-platform attribution path." [The New Era of Meta Ads Has Officially Begun]

## 8. Content Ad Strategies (his signature frameworks)

Core principle: **replicate the organic sales process via paid ads**, compressing the sales-cycle timeline. Direct response is always safest/cheapest (fewest steps); content strategies add friction/cost but lift show rates, close rates, and sales-team feedback. [The Top Ad Strategies Working Right Now For Hitting $1M/Mo]

**Baseline stats he uses:** average **~1.8 seconds** spent per post; a person scrolls **~356 feet** of content per day. [The Top Ad Strategies]

### Venus Fly Trap (1.0 → 2.0) — ~13–14 yrs old
- Build a **sequence of content** gated by a **10-second view threshold** (10 sec = ~5× the attention of a normal post): watch 10 sec of video 1 → unlock video 2 → 10 sec → video 3 → then (and only then) show direct-response ads. [The Top Ad Strategies]
- Target the *same cold audiences* you'd use for DR ads. The videos are just content — no CTAs.
- **2.0 change:** **5 videos per step** (a "content cycle bin") instead of 1 — gives Andromeda/Lattice more context to distribute, and a second chance if someone ignores one video.

### The Forester (CPM-lowering)
- **Content cycle bin #1:** 10–50 pieces of content run as **engagement/video-view campaigns** to cold audiences; a mere **3-second view** makes them retargetable → DR ads then cost far less on a CPM basis. [The Top Ad Strategies]
- **Content cycle bin #2:** (intentional content — case studies, testimonials, podcasts, demos, how-tos) shown *alongside* the DR ads to the 3-second viewers who haven't converted. Lifts awareness + lowers total cost.

### Harvester vs. Tornado (at-scale)
Based on Chet Holmes' buyer pyramid (Haynes' split ≈ 33/33/33): **in-market** (~3–4%, best ROI, just use direct response), **needs-convinced** (~9%), **mass market** (~30%+), and **non-buyers** (never spend on them). [The Top Ad Strategies]
- **Harvester** → mass-market audiences who don't know you (multiple cycle bins, 50–200 pieces each).
- **Tornado** → needs-convinced demographic when you have an excess of organic content.

### Hammer Them (back-end content blitz)
Bolt-on after a key action (books a call / opts into webinar / buys low-ticket / DMs you) but **before** the call/webinar happens. [The Top Ad Strategies] / [New Facebook Ads Algorithm Update Is Killing Sales]
- Load **30–50 short-form + 15–50 long-form** pieces. Audience min ~**150 people**.
- Goal: **15–20 frequency across UNIQUE pieces** (never one piece 15–20 times).
- Mechanic: **one piece of content per ad set + a 3-second video-view exclusion** so once they watch 3 sec they rotate to the next piece. If one piece shows a too-high frequency, people aren't passing 3 sec on it — "it's a junker," pull it.
- Loads it with **unique-mechanism + process content**; result = better-framed leads, higher show rates/close rates, less education burden on closers.

### The 5 Back-End Selling Systems
1. **Confirmation page** best practices (incl. **breakout videos** — ~5–20 clips of ~1–3 min each answering a lead's likely objection/question). [New Facebook Ads Algorithm Update Is Killing Sales] / [The Top Ad Strategies]
2. **Value-dense email sequences**
3. **Setter pre-call** best practices
4. **Manipulate AI to say good things about you** (buyers outsource critical thinking to AI)
5. **Hammer Them** ad strategy

### Reverse Fly Trap
Lead with **direct response**, then remarket with more DR **plus** a **content cycle bin** (engagement/video-view optimized, wide range of unique-mechanism content). Unlike Hammer Them, it has no aggressive frequency goal — it just keeps relevant content available at every funnel stage. In this new algorithm, also run a deliberate **standard retargeting campaign** (not relied on in recent years, now worth rebuilding). [New Facebook Ads Algorithm Update Is Killing Sales]

## 9. Instagram DM Ads (2026)

Haynes calls DM ads "one of the best and absolute worst funnels" — unstable, easily rug-pulled, prone to pixel-conditioning rot — but people do hit $1M/mo with them. [The Best Way To Run Instagram DM Ads In 2026]

### The core problem
- Optimizing around `Message` (engagement/lead campaign) or `Follow`/comment tricks → **donkeys condition your pixel**. "All it takes is one donkey to kick the door in."
- Typical ratio when you "raw dog" the message objective: **~80% unqualified / ~20% qualified** hitting the results column → the algo hunts more donkeys, and performance "perpetually goes down" across relaunches.
- **Comparison-bias trap:** a "$10 cost per DM" where only 10 of 50 are qualified is really a **$50 cost per qualified DM**. When you fix optimization and the results column *shows* $50, that's the real (always-true) cost, not a worse one. "Get the turds out of the punch bowl."

### The goal
**Flip the ratio** so ~70–80% of events hitting the results column are qualified — fire a **lead-type standard event only after a qualifier**, not on the raw DM.

### Path 1 — Quiz funnel ("brokie bait")
1. Ad → simple landing page with **1–2 qualifier questions**.
2. Conditional logic: answer correctly → page 3 with a **mini-chat button** that sends them into IG DMs with your trigger word.
3. **Fire a lead standard event** (`Lead` / `Schedule` / `Submit Application`) on the qualifying page via web pixel. [The Best Way To Run Instagram DM Ads In 2026]
4. Then the normal DM flow: setter (or AI) convo → booked call → show → back-end selling systems → high AOV.
- Use a **trigger word specific to ads** (so you can distinguish ad DMs from organic).

### Path 2 — Lead campaign + Schedule optimization (the "pro" trick)
- Campaign objective = **Lead**; ad-set optimization = **Schedule**.
- The **ad copy says "DM us [word]"**, but the ad's **destination URL = the scheduler page** your DM setters push people to.
- When the person eventually DMs, the setter sends that same scheduler URL; on booking they hit a **confirmation page where the `Schedule` standard event fires** (also use **Conversion API**).
- Effect: the pixel conditions around **qualified people who actually booked calls**, and the results column shows a real **cost per qualified call**. A few unqualified people slip through, but it's no longer the majority. [The Best Way To Run Instagram DM Ads In 2026]

> Standard-event TAM caveat he reinforces here: out of a 1,000,000-person audience you only reach the slice (~100K) probable to do the event you optimized for before frequency spikes. [The Best Way To Run Instagram DM Ads In 2026]

## 10. Common Mistakes (the brutally honest list)

From [10 Years of Brutally Honest Facebook Ads Advice] unless noted:
1. **It's you, not the algorithm.** Blaming Zuckerberg kills your creativity to solve it (ref: *Extreme Ownership*, Jocko).
2. **Seeking data that validates failure** instead of data on why it works (e.g., a $12M/mo company citing "CPMs are higher" articles — CPMs always rise over time).
3. **Over-reliance on the pixel = your messaging sucks.**
4. **Bad messaging attracts the wrong people consistently** — then people blame the pixel/algo.
5. **Selling to poor people = every KPI must be near-perfect** (CPM, CTR, page CR, cost/lead, show rate, close rate, AOV). Low AOV puts max pressure on every step.
6. **Your offer price sucks** if stats are bad across the board.
7. **Reputation hits ROAS** — bad Trustpilot / 1-star reviews / Reddit threads correlate with (e.g.) a 28% show rate.
8. **You genuinely don't know what you're doing** (placebo'd by yourself or by the 20th mediocre agency).
9. **Be right in reality, not in opinion** — reality is the feedback mechanism; if results are bad, you're wrong, change.
10. **Over-testing for your budget** — at a few hundred $/day, run one broad cold ad set and <10–20 best creatives, not 30,000 creatives / 5 ad sets.
11. **"It didn't work then" ≠ it won't work now** — e.g., a brand stuck 8 months because a December scale attempt dipped ROAS (December = holidays). Beware "company narrative."
12. **If you're not getting results, you're wrong about what you're sure you're right about** (funnel, sales team, ads, everything).
13. **Pixels only carry 180 days / recency bias** (repeated as its own mistake).
14. **It's gambling — but with better odds.** Learn the **data hierarchy**: top = collected $/booked call, ROAS; these are *made of* CPM, CTR, page CR, show rate, close rate, AOV. Don't get lost in low-impact stats.
15. **Simplification matters enormously.** He cut an account from **70+ near-identical campaigns to 3** → cost per result dropped ~200% and ROAS nearly 4×'d. [10 Years of Brutally Honest Facebook Ads Advice]
16. **(Bonus) Where something is winning is where it's most probable to keep winning** — don't move winners from a test campaign into a scaling campaign.

Supporting frame from [82 Minutes Of The Top Paid Advertising Strategies]: **advertising is gambling until you build an edge** (right ad strategies + funnel + offer + team). Agency deals need margins **≥50%**; call-funnel structure = VSL → qualifying application → scheduler → "hammer the [hell] out of people in between with content and emails" to create layups for closers.

## 12. Quick-Reference Decision Rules

1. **Launch** with dialed-in, "sensitive" messaging + the right standard event. Messaging does the targeting first.
2. **Trust Meta's distribution** (broad + many ads per ad set) unless you have a specific reason not to — then run the **Thunderdome**.
3. **Post-Andromeda:** diversify by *reason to buy*, not by swapping hooks. Prefer **Path 2 (~15 whole-unique ads/month)**; load **25–30 unique per ad set**; expect 1–3 to carry it; refresh by duplicating and turning off the winners.
4. **Pixel conditioning** is a last layer, not a crutch. Wrong pocket → withhold data / Conversion API / new pixel; right pocket → flood the results column.
5. **Scale where winners already win.** Never split test vs. scale campaigns. Keep winners at their ceiling as foundational campaigns.
6. **Simplify** the account. Fewer campaigns, less overlap.
7. Against the **competitor swarm**: creative range + unique-mechanism/process content + shorter funnel + back-end selling systems + be everywhere.
8. **DM ads:** never optimize on raw `Message`. Gate with a qualifier (quiz funnel or lead→schedule trick) so only qualified events condition the pixel.
